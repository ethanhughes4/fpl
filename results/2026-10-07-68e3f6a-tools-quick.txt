--- Q10 run 1: What does the Bench Boost chip do?
**Bench Boost** is one of the FPL chips. In the gameweek you play it, your four bench players' points count toward your total along with your starting eleven. So all 15 players score for you that week, not just 11.

Key points:

- **It lasts one gameweek.** It runs from that gameweek's deadline until the end of the gameweek.
- **Your bench order doesn't matter** that week, because every bench player scores anyway. Auto-substitutions don't happen either, since nobody is waiting on the bench to come on.
- **Only one chip per gameweek.** You can't combine it with Triple Captain, Free Hit or Wildcard in the same week. A common move is to Wildcard the week before to build a strong 15, then Bench Boost the next week.
- **Best time to use it:** a **double gameweek** where many of your 15 players have two fixtures, ideally with all of them fit and likely to start. A weak bench (non-playing goalkeeper, rotation risks) wastes most of the value.
- **How many you get:** in recent seasons there have been two Bench Boosts, one for each half of the season. Check the official FPL rules for this season's exact allocation and deadlines.

I can't activate chips for you; that has to be done on the FPL site or app. I can pull your brief for this week if you want to see how strong your bench looks right now.
