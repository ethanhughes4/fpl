- **It lasts one gameweek.** You turn it on before that gameweek's deadline. Your bench goes back to normal after it.

- **You can cancel it before the deadline** if you change your mind.
- **You can't play another chip in the same gameweek.** For example, you can't combine it with Triple Captain or Free Hit.
- **How many you get:** In recent seasons, managers have had two Bench Boosts, one for each half of the season. This can change from season to season, so check this season's rules in the FPL app.

- **Double gameweeks.** Many of your 15 players play twice, so the bench can score a lot.
- **When your bench is strong.** All four bench players should be fit, likely to start and facing good fixtures. Managers often spend a transfer or two, or a Wildcard or Free Hit the week before, to set this up.
- **Not when your bench is weak.** If your bench players are injured or won't start, the chip is mostly wasted.
