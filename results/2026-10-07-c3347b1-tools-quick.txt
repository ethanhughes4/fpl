--- Q10 run 1: What does the Bench Boost chip do?
In Fantasy Premier League, **Bench Boost** counts your bench players' points for one gameweek, on top of your starting eleven's.

- **All 15 players score.** Normally only your starting XI count. With Bench Boost, your 4 substitutes (including the backup goalkeeper) add their points too.
- **It lasts one gameweek.** It applies only to the gameweek you play it in.
- **You can't use two chips in the same gameweek.** For example, you can't pair it with Triple Captain or Free Hit.
- **It doesn't change captaincy.** Your captain still gets double points as usual.
- **How many you get:** Recent seasons have given two Bench Boosts, one for each half of the season. Check this season's rules in the FPL app to be sure.
- **Cancelling:** You can turn it off before the gameweek deadline. After the deadline, it's locked in.

**When to use it:** It works best in a **double gameweek**, when many teams play twice. Your bench players can then score twice too. It also helps to use a free transfer or Wildcard first so that all 15 players have good fixtures and are likely to start. A bench player who doesn't play scores nothing, even with the chip active.
